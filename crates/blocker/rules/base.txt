! Vel — built-in blocklist (Adblock Plus syntax)
!
! Deliberately small. Every rule here becomes a trigger WebKit evaluates
! against every request, so this list is tuned for the ratio that matters on
! a video-first browser: bytes and connections saved per rule, not raw
! coverage. Point Vel at EasyList if you want exhaustive filtering; this is
! the floor that makes YouTube and Twitch load fast out of the box.
!
! Hard rule for anything added here: NEVER match a media delivery host.
! googlevideo.com, *.ttvnw.net, video-edge-*, and the Akamai/Fastly edges
! carry the actual video. A rule that catches one of them trades ads for a
! permanent spinner. The `no_rule_touches_media_delivery` test in lib.rs
! enforces this.
